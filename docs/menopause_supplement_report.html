11What this report cannot tell you

  • Transcription quality is unvalidated. The transcripts were produced by the previous pipeline's audio-visual model, and the source video files no longer exist, so they cannot be re-transcribed or checked against the audio. Word error rate is unknown.
  • No visual signal. Text on screen, product packaging and gesture are invisible to this pipeline. For short-form video, on-screen text often carries the product name and the claim. Re-running with --include-visual measures what that costs.
  • 205 videos have no codable evidence and were excluded rather than labelled. The previous pipeline labelled them anyway.
  • Machine labels are not validated against humans yet. The agreement statistics that would license inferential claims need the human-coded sample (sample_for_coding.py) to be completed first. Until then every number here is descriptive.
  • Nothing here says whether a claim is scientifically correct. The pipeline records what evidence a post offers. Comparing those claims against the clinical literature is a separate piece of work and deliberately out of scope.
  • Corpus construction is not random. These are search results, shaped by search terms and platform algorithms, so prevalence figures describe this corpus rather than social media as a whole.
