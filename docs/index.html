Menopause Supplement Pipeline

How 18,137 Google search results become 3,881 unique menopause-targeted posts that mention supplements, classified by content type, supplement category and symptom target. Search links are those collected by 28 Jul 2026, the last update before the videos were downloaded; counts as of 25 November 2026.
